Methodology

How research becomes
production software.

Each Sentinel module follows the same rigorous path from academic research to enterprise-ready SaaS — ensuring every claim is independently verifiable.

01
Hypothesis
Each module begins with a clearly defined research question — validated against real-world domain problems in healthcare, security, or environmental monitoring.
  • Literature review and gap analysis
  • Domain expert consultation
  • Problem formulation and scoping
02
Experiment
Models are developed, tested, and iterated on industry-standard benchmark datasets — not cherry-picked proprietary data.
  • CICIDS-2017 and UNSW-NB15 for network
  • Real-world ocean noise datasets for PAM
  • Cross-validated model evaluation
03
Publish
Findings are submitted to peer-reviewed journals indexed in major scientific databases — subject to independent expert review before publication.
  • Submitted to indexed journals
  • Blind peer review process
  • Revisions and final publication
04
Deploy
Only after peer review and production validation does the methodology get packaged into a Sentinel module and shipped to enterprise customers.
  • Production-hardened engineering
  • API-first architecture
  • Enterprise security & compliance layer
